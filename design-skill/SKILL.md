# Anteiku design skill

Open `README.md` in this folder and follow its instructions to the letter: read `01`–`09`,
then `reference/docs/`, look at `screens/` and `art/` with your own eyes, skim `reference/code/`,
and run your own web research on AI slop in design and text, current non-cringe Russian and English
youth slang, premium UX writing and the latest Telegram Bot API changelog before you design anything.

All generated artwork of the family lives next to this folder in `../asset-gallery/`.
